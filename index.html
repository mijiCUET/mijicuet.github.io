Welcome

Mathematics is how we read the world.

Everything around you obeys a pattern — the turning of the seasons, the shape of a honeycomb, the arc of a thrown ball, the way a bridge holds itself up. Mathematics is the language those patterns are written in, and learning it is learning to read the universe. Every question on this page is built fresh the moment you press start, so you can practise a topic a hundred times and never meet the same question twice.

Practice and test progress is stored in the private account repository through the secure backend. Do not enter personal information.

Why it matters

Small ideas that explain enormous things

◑ Why the Moon does not fall It is falling — and moving sideways fast enough to keep missing the Earth. One equation describes both an apple and a moon.
⬡ Why bees build hexagons Of all the shapes that tile a flat surface with no gaps, the hexagon walls off the most space for the least wax.
△ How we measured the Earth Eratosthenes used two shadows, one well and a triangle — and got the planet's size close to right, over 2,000 years ago.
★ How we reached the Moon Katherine Johnson checked the flight paths by hand. Arithmetic you are learning now carried people through space and home again.
